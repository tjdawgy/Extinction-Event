Run: `node audit/sim/lanes6e.js --out audit/logs-p1/lanes6e-base` on v2.94's build: the lane-map half of the persona-balanced
self-play (Two-Lane and Three-Lane, every ordered faction pairing, every personality pair, the selfplay-6c seeds; 108
matches, 0 errors). Every result is identical to batch 6d's run (selfplay6d-b3): the Axiarchs 6-23-7 on Two-Lane and 6-26-4
on Three-Lane, 17% on each. Every 10 game seconds each side's ground army value per lane (by position and by its units'
lane), the enemy's per lane, its warp point and the middle of its ground army (distance from its HQ); every unit lost with
its value, age, the value of its own and enemy units within 300, and where the warp point stood. Tables:
`node audit/sim/lanes6e-report.js audit/logs-p1/lanes6e-base` (report.md) and `node audit/sim/lanes6e-a1.js
audit/logs-p1/lanes6e-base` (below).

## 2. What does happen: Axiarch units die alone, fresh from a warp-in

| faction | map | value lost / match | died alone (no own unit within 300) | in a small group (< 3.5× its value) | of the alone: under 60 s old | of the alone: while holding (retreat stance) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Last Fist | Two-Lane | 20,703 | 4% | 19% | 40% | 42% |
| Bloomhost | Two-Lane | 19,731 | 5% | 14% | 44% | 50% |
| Axiarchs | Two-Lane | 21,004 | **18%** | 27% | **83%** | 72% |
| Last Fist | Three-Lane | 22,121 | 4% | 16% | 32% | 33% |
| Bloomhost | Three-Lane | 17,127 | 6% | 15% | 55% | 58% |
| Axiarchs | Three-Lane | 15,207 | **20%** | 26% | **73%** | 77% |

Outnumbered losses (own value within 300 under 2/3 of the enemy's there): the Axiarchs 42% / 47% of their lost value (Two /
Three-Lane), Last Fist 20% / 19%, Bloomhost 35% / 36%; of it while their whole army was at least the enemy's ("piecemeal"
in the strict sense): 8% / 4%, the others 6-11%. They are outnumbered where they die, not split while ahead.

**The cause: the warp point.** Every Axiarch unit warps in at its side's warp point, by default (`defaultWarp`) the front edge
of the frontmost power-field patch. On the lane maps the frontmost patch is the centre node's or a forward pad's, half way
down the map:

| Axiarchs | warp point from own HQ (median) | middle of the ground army from own HQ (median) | warp point 400+ ahead of the army's middle | lone deaths: median distance from the warp point |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Two-Lane | 999 | 738 | 35% of the time | 387 |
| Three-Lane | 1,049 | 458 | 45% of the time | 374 |

So while the army holds near home (retreat stance), each new unit appears at the centre node, 500-600 ahead of it and often in
the enemy's path, and dies there alone within a minute (traced matches: Shardlings and Lancers 3-60 s old, 100-280 from the
warp point, at 1,400-1,500 from their HQ while the army's middle stood at 400). On Classic the field and nodes sit close to
the bases (nodes at 680/850/1,020 of a shorter lane), so the gap is small: the Axiarchs win 50% there.

## 4. A second fault, every faction's AI: a base attack walks past the base

An all-in base attack with no push point walks to the map's edge behind the enemy base (units.js: `end` = 0 or L). Units there
stand out of sight of what the enemy rebuilds behind them. The middle of the ground army stood past the enemy HQ in 6% (Two-
Lane) and 7% (Three-Lane) of the Axiarchs' samples (Last Fist 0%, Bloomhost 0-1%: their attacks end the base sooner); in a traced
A2 trial a 3× Axiarch army stood there 12 minutes and the match was drawn by the storm. Reported for every faction; changed for
the Axiarchs only (A2).

## Conclusion for A2

The fix that answers the data is to keep new units with the army: the Axiarch warp point (an existing teleport, the player's
"Set warp point") moves with the army instead of sitting at the frontmost field patch. The requested one-army-one-lane rule is
kept too (the lane AI's split is small, but its surplus shifts and Three-Lane's second capture group do take units away), and a
base attack ends at the enemy HQ. No other faction is changed: Last Fist and Bloomhost keep about 90% of their army in one lane
and lose 4-6% of their value alone.
