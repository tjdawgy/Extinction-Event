# Batch 6d, Part B1: why Last Fist wins (diagnosis on the b6ai build, no change yet)

**Data.** The A3 run (v2.94, b6ai on, b6d off, every other switch at its default): 162 persona-balanced Hard vs Hard matches, 0
errors. Faction win rates: Last Fist 83-18-7, 77% (68-84); Bloomhost 44-55-9, 41% (32-50); Axiarchs 23-77-8, 21% (15-30). Tables:
[diag-b1.md](diag-b1.md) (audit/sim/diag6d-b1.js: curves, decision times, the damage matrix, per-unit damage and killed value per
100 spent); Salvage from the 68 Last Fist sides recorded after the Salvage counter was added. Medians per side unless stated.

## The causes, ranked

### 1. Last Fist simply puts more into its army: it earns more and spends more of it on units (volume, not efficiency)

| | Last Fist | Bloomhost | Axiarchs |
|---|---|---|---|
| earned per minute (credits + energy) | **2,087** | 1,759 | 1,780 |
| share of earnings spent on units | **73%** | 60% | 55% |
| spent on units per side (whole run) | 23,105 | 15,823 | 14,010 |
| 1,000+ credits banked | 12% of the match | 5% | 22% |

- **Salvage is 12.1% of Last Fist's earnings** (median 3,208 credits + 1,508 energy a match, 191 credits a minute; every Hard
  Last Fist builds the Salvage Yard, `HARD_SIG_7C`), plus a median **14 Overdrive starts** (production +25% for 20 s each). Without
  Salvage it still earns 1,926 a minute, 9% more than the others: two thirds of its income edge is Salvage.
- Paired in the same match, Last Fist out-spends on units by 32% against both: 25,304 against Bloomhost's 19,151 per side, 19,694
  against the Axiarchs' 14,876.
- **Per credit it trades worse**: killed value per 100 spent on units, Last Fist 71 (vs Bloomhost) and 69 (vs Axiarchs); Bloomhost
  104 and the Axiarchs 101 against Last Fist. It wins by having more, earlier.
- When it wins, its army lead is never lost from a median 5:00 (Bloomhost) and 3:00 (Axiarchs); its army is +507 at 4:00 and
  +2,010 at 10:00 against Bloomhost, +405 and +2,065 against the Axiarchs; income +8 to +9 a second at 4:00-5:00.

### 2. The Tank carries that army through both opponents' front lines and bases

- The Tank is Last Fist's most-funded unit (542,755 spent, 1,487 trained, every side) and does **24% of its damage in matches it
  wins** (Marines 14%, Heavies 11%, Titans and Artillery 10% each); 242 damage per 100 spent.
- It is the top dealer against both: 21% of Last Fist's damage against Bloomhost (49% of it into Bulwarks), 25% against the
  Axiarchs (24% into Bastions), and the top building-breaker (3,574 and 4,386 damage to buildings per side).

### 3. The Axiarchs cannot turn their income into an army

- **Energy-bound:** their units are 25.9% energy by cost (Last Fist 19.4%, Bloomhost 18.9%) on the same Reactors and Refineries;
  snapshots show them on 900-1,300 credits with 26-109 energy. They bank 1,000+ for 22% of the match, production stands idle 44%
  of it, and only 55% of their earnings becomes units. Peak army: **14 units** (Last Fist 35, Bloomhost 29).
- **Their most-funded units fail their role:** the Shardling (front-line melee, 344,720 spent, the most of any Axiarch unit) does
  78 damage per 100 spent and the Riftstalker (diver, 197,600 spent) 64; together 39% of Axiarch unit spending for 12% of their
  damage in wins (the Lancer: 267 per 100 spent, 30%).
- On the lane maps it is worse: the Axiarchs win 5 of 36 on Two-Lane and 3 of 36 on Three-Lane (15 of 36 on Classic).

### 4. Artillery: the most efficient unit any faction masses, with no answer at range

- Artillery does **400 damage per 100 spent** (643 trained in 52 sides; 94 killed value per 100), above every other unit bought
  in volume (Sniper 362, Lancer 267, Titan 262, Tank 242, Spitter 240) and above 6b's target of about 300. Its damage goes into
  the front liners (Bulwarks 42%, Bastions 33%) from beyond their reach, the owner's playtest finding.

**Bloomhost:** it loses mostly to Last Fist (7-43-4) and beats the Axiarchs (37-12-5). Its weak buys are the Bilecaster (64 per
100 spent on 203,040 spent, 4% of its winning damage), the Dreadthorn (57) and the Needlewing (82); its Bulwark soaks most of Last
Fist's damage (49-58% of the Tank's and Heavy's) and still does 165 per 100 spent, so it holds its role. Bloomhost's gap is cause
1 and 2 (it is out-spent by 32% and its front line is broken by the Tank), not one failing unit.

## Levers this points to (Part B2, at most four)

1. Last Fist's Salvage Yard (the 12% income edge and Overdrive): bring the Yard's Salvage share down.
2. The Tank (Last Fist's carrier): a little less damage.
3. The Axiarchs: their most-funded unit, the Shardling, failing its front-line role; or their energy squeeze.
4. Artillery: lower its salvo (deploy and telegraph untouched).
6c's tuning (Bastion barrier, brood cost, Leviathan bombs, Leaper latch, Riftstalker blade) is not where B1 points: none of those
units is among the causes above (the Riftstalker is weak, but 6c already raised it; it is left alone).
