LAST FIST — IDENTITY AND SIGNATURE MECHANIC

Identity: "Humanity refuses to die." The battered remnants of humanity, years into a losing war. Technologically exhausted, not primitive: tanks, gunships, orbital weapons and shields, but worn, welded, patched and retrofitted. Gritty, stubborn, industrial, resourceful. Motto: "Weld it. Load it. Hold the line."

Gameplay identity: the adaptable combined-arms faction and the easiest to learn. Keep the existing units, trees (Barracks: Mass Deployment / Specialists; War Factory: Siege / Assault; Tech Lab: Air Power / Support) and abilities (Emergency Shield, Repair Pulse, Orbital Strike). No new branches.

Signature mechanic — Salvage: Last Fist recovers resources from every unit destroyed near its forces, its own losses included. That makes it stubborn in long fights rather than a snowball. Three-faction symmetry: Bloomhost recovers health (creep regen), Axiarchs recover shields, Last Fist recovers resources.

Visual direction (v2.32–v2.33):
- Palette: steel gray, dark military green, burnt orange, muted yellow, charcoal, warning-stripe accents. Team colors must stay clearly readable, including in Last Fist mirror matches.
- Details: rivets, welded armor patches, hydraulics, pipes and cables, external ammo, exhaust, warning lights, sparks, visible wear and repairs. Practical, not sleek.
- Building tiers must be real physical transformations, not color changes. T1 Operational: hastily assembled, exposed machinery. T2 Reinforced: added plating, supports, more machinery. T3 Fully militarized: bulky, heavy armor, visible weapon systems. Priority: HQ, War Factory, Turret.
- Units, weapons, explosions and sound should feel like a desperate but capable human military.
- Presentation only: no stat, cost, AI or balance changes in those batches. Cheap to draw on a phone.

Rejected: Fortify (overlaps Emergency Shield, adds a button for little gain); salvage from buildings (snowballs).

Build order (one batch per session):
1. v2.30 Salvage.
2. v2.31 Polish: Axiarch weapon sounds, the flaky Aggressor test, the ability banner overlap.
3. v2.32 Last Fist buildings visual refresh (T1–T3).
4. v2.33 Last Fist units, effects and sound.
Then the strategy explorer and full audit.
