# Batch 6d, Part A1: why the Turtle and the Economist lose (report only, no game change)

**Data.** v2.93's persona-balanced self-play re-run with per-side time series (audit/sim/selfplay-6c.js → audit6a.js, which
since v2.94 also records income, army value, bank and supply once a game minute; seconds of idle production, of 1,000+ banked,
of attack stance; the AI's strategy moves). 162 Hard vs Hard matches, every switch on, 0 errors; **162 of 162 identical to 6c's
run** (audit/logs-p1/selfplay6c-b2, same winner and length), so the instrumentation changes nothing. Tables:
[diag-a1.md](diag-a1.md) (audit/sim/diag6d-a1.js), [report6c.md](report6c.md). Snapshot traces: [traces.txt](traces.txt).

**Win rates (v2.93):** Boomer 73-23-12, 68% (58-76); Economist 36-60-12, 33% (25-43); Turtle 37-63-8, 34% (26-44) (6c's A1 on
v2.92: 71 / 36 / 30%). Against the Boomer: Economist **1-32-3**, Turtle **7-26-3**. Last Fist's Boomer 33-2-1.

**Correction to 6c's "natural mix".** 6c weighted the Axiarchs' Hard pool as 1/3 Boomer, 2/3 Turtle. The code's pool has been
Boomer, Boomer, Turtle since v2.43 (src/sim/ai-presets.js:111, `AI_PRESETS.hard.byFac.axiarchs.kinds`). With the true pools,
v2.93's normal-play figures are Last Fist 60% (47-72), Bloomhost 40% (26-55), Axiarchs 29% (18-44); 6c's 19% for the Axiarchs
came from the wrong weighting. audit/sim/selfplay-6c-report.js now uses the true pools (and equal thirds when b6ai is on).

## Findings, with evidence

| personality | first field push | first base attack | lost before ever attacking the base | idle production | 1,000+ banked | attack stance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Boomer | 3:34 | 4:01 | 2 of 23 | 40% of the match | 13% | 54% |
| Economist | 4:00 | 4:39 | 4 of 60 | 56% | 12% | 35% |
| Turtle | 4:22 | 6:00 | 8 of 63 | 65% | 3% | 30% |

Income (credits + energy per second, median) at 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 minutes: Boomer 24 / 38 / 42 / 43 / 41; Economist 16 / 26 /
35 / 35 / 34; **Turtle 14 / 14 / 22 / 27 / 29**. Army value at 4 / 6 / 8 minutes: Boomer 1,380 / 1,994 / 2,645; Economist 743 /
1,636 / 1,905; Turtle 400 / 985 / 1,460. Paired against a Boomer in the same match (own minus the Boomer's): Economist income
-14 at 4:00 to -19 at 10:00, army -825 at 4:00, -1,895 at 8:00; Turtle income -24 from 4:00 on, army -948 at 4:00, -1,599 at 8:00.

1. **Both are out-earned from minute 2 and never catch up: too few Refineries, upgraded too late.** Median Refineries built:
   Boomer 3, Economist 2, Turtle 2. Hard's Refinery cap is 2 (`DIFF.hard.refineries`) plus the personality's `refDelta`: the
   Boomer's is 1, the Economist's and Turtle's 0, so **the Economist's plan names three Refineries and the cap cuts the third**: the
   personality meant to out-earn earns the same per second as the Turtle early and less than the Boomer all match. First tier 2
   (a Refinery for the Boomer): Boomer 0:59, Economist 2:04, Turtle 2:32; first tier 3: 3:19, 3:41, **6:42**. Trace (Classic,
   Axiarch Turtle vs Last Fist Boomer, seed 610): at 6:00 the Turtle's two Refineries are still T1 (11 credits/s) while it has
   already paid for a T2 Turret, a T2 HQ and a signature building; the Boomer makes 29 credits/s from three T2 Refineries.
2. **Neither fields an army in time; the Boomer's first base attack lands while they are at half its size.** The Boomer attacks
   the base at 4:01 with 1,380 army against the Economist's 743 and the Turtle's 400. The Economist's losses come early (25 of
   60 before 10:00, median length 10:49); in the seed-605 trace (Bloomhost Economist vs Last Fist Boomer) it has two Refineries,
   a Turret and 15 supply at 4:00, and by 6:00 only its HQ is left.
3. **Stalls: idle production, not floating money.** Production stands idle 56% (Economist) and 65% (Turtle) of the match against
   the Boomer's 40%. The cause is a lack of credits and energy, not banking: the Turtle has 1,000+ credits only 3% of the time
   (median bank 73-117 between 4:00 and 6:00), and its supply at 6:00 is 19/28 (Boomer 33/34). The Economist does float (12%;
   median bank 616-784 between 6:00 and 8:00) while sitting near its supply cap (24/28 at 6:00): money it can't turn into army.
   The AI's `calm` rule (no upgrades while an enemy is near its base) holds the defensive personalities' economy and supply
   upgrades through every Boomer push.
4. **Defending too long (the Turtle).** Its first base attack comes at 6:00 (two minutes after the Boomer's); it spends 30% of
   the match on attack stance (Boomer 54%) and 8 of its 63 losses come without ever attacking the base. Its persona waits for a
   1.2-1.4× edge (`attackEdge`), a 1.1-1.3× wave and a deadline of 11:00-14:00 before it must attack; by then the Boomer's
   economy has doubled its own.
5. **Faction spread.** Per faction the same story (win rate; median income at 6:00; first base attack): Last Fist Boomer 33/36,
   43, 4:00 vs Turtle 18/36, 23, 5:25; Bloomhost Boomer 20/36, 43 vs Turtle 9/36, 19, 6:01; Axiarchs Boomer 20/36 vs Economist
   7/36 and Turtle 10/36. Last Fist's Economist and Turtle still reach 50-53% (its units win fights the other two cannot).

**In short:** the Boomer is the only Hard personality whose economy is built for Hard's pace. The Economist is capped below its
own plan and spends early credits on a Turret; the Turtle invests in defences before its economy, never reaches the Boomer's
income, and waits too long to attack. Both stop upgrading whenever an enemy is near, which against a Boomer is most of the match.
