## Reading

The conversion did what it was asked (energy 20.0% of Axiarch unit spending; credits banked over 1,000 for 15% of a match
instead of 25%) but not what it was for: the Axiarchs went from 45% to 34% with it alone and to 39% with the Riftstalker's
shields. From 8:00 they are now short of credits with energy unspent (415-1,381 banked from 10:00), and their money moved from
the Bastion and Prismwing to the units the old energy price held back (Marksman, Riftstalker, Severance, Gravemantle,
Wraithwing), none of which trades better. Their target (42% or more overall, 40% on each lane map) is not met: 39% (30-48)
overall, 39% on Two-Lane, 31% on Three-Lane. The B1 allowance (one more Axiarch change) is spent on the Riftstalker. The next
lever is the conversion itself: a share near 24% (between v2.94's 26% and 20%), or the old prices with b6elane alone (45%).
