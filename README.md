# Extinction Event

A single-file mobile real-time strategy game. Open `index.html` in a browser (best in landscape on a phone).

## Test Range (v2.81)

A sandbox on the real game engine, from **Test Range** on the main menu: place units of any faction for blue or red (×1, ×3, ×5, ×10), Turrets and Refineries (each faction's own), or one-tap enemy groups (Infantry blob, Armor, Swarm, Elite, Air); then Start, Pause, Reset (the same setup and seed, replayed exactly), Clear and 0.5× / 1× / 2×. Each side Attacks or Holds; red can be told not to fight back; "Control blue myself" gives blue the game's own controls (docs/CONTROLS.md); Compare splits the field into up to three rows that can't touch (the same enemy, a different attacker in each). Each row reads out damage, kills and when a side was wiped out. Tap a unit for its health, shields and state, and Remove. Up to five setups save by name on the device. Never saved as a match and never recorded in stats or history. Code: `src/sim/testrange.js` (SIM side), `src/ui/testrange.js`. The faction rework it serves: [docs/FACTION_REWORK.md](docs/FACTION_REWORK.md).

## Faction rework, batch 2a (v2.82)

The first three siege units of [docs/FACTION_REWORK.md](docs/FACTION_REWORK.md) "Batch 2", behind one switch block stored per game (`REWORK` → `g.rw`, src/sim/rework.js; on by default; each Test Range row sets its own under Setup → Rework switches):
- **Artillery** (Last Fist): still the only true artillery. **Deploy** (`artDeploy`): it deploys (2 s) before firing and packs up (1.5 s) before moving, automatically; stabiliser legs fold down when deployed. **Telegraphed shells** (`artTelegraph`): every shell lands where it was aimed, at least 1.6 s later, and a team-coloured ring with a closing inner circle marks the spot; units that move out take nothing (v2.82 had AI units step out of markers; removed in v2.83).
- **Gorebore** (Bloomhost, replaces the Bile Lobber; Evolution Chamber, Burrowing Evolution T2): burrows whenever it moves with burrow ready (speed 40, untargetable, leaves a dirt trail anyone with vision sees), erupts under its target (70 within 45; backline ranged units first), stays up 4 s with a bite, burrows again 10 s after the eruption.
- **Severance** (Axiarchs, replaces the Rift Mortar; Architectum, Rupture T2): every 12 s a slow rift orb (range 300, speed 120) exiles every enemy unit within 45 for 4 s after 20 damage: frozen, untouchable, timers paused, then 6 s immune. A weak bolt in between.
With `b2a` off a game plays exactly as v2.81 (the pinned determinism fingerprints match). Tests: `tests/rework-2a.test.js`; screenshots: `node tests/rework-2a-shots.js` → docs/screenshots/rework2a-*.png.

## Faction rework, batch 2b (v2.83)

The last three siege units of [docs/FACTION_REWORK.md](docs/FACTION_REWORK.md) "Batch 2", behind `REWORK.b2b` (stored per game in `g.rw` like batch 2a; on by default; a Test Range row's switch is under Setup → Rework switches; code in src/sim/rework-2b.js, art in src/render/units-rework-2b.js). With `b2b` off the three play exactly as in v2.82.
- **Titan** (Last Fist): walking cover. Last Fist infantry within 70 of a Titan take 30% less damage from anything not melee (several Titans don't add up; a faint shimmer over each covered unit, a faint ring under the Titan). Its cannon reaches 160 and fires on the move. Next to an enemy building (within 100) it plants by itself (2 s; claws dig in, a front plate lowers), hits buildings 2.5× while planted, and unplants (1.5 s) when ordered away or 2 s after the last building.
- **Ruinmaw** (Bloomhost): a living tunnel. 1500 HP, heavy armour, no ranged attack, no acid. Whenever it moves it is underground (speed 30, can't be targeted, hurt or exiled; a wide dirt trail shows), and it surfaces where it stops: send it with a normal move order. Surfaced it is a gate: select Bloomhost ground units and tap **Tunnel** in the command bar; they walk to the entrance beside your Hive (in the Test Range, your starting edge), go in, and 3 s later pour out of its mouth, one every 0.5 s. Moving it sends anyone inside back out at the entrance; if it dies, everyone inside dies. Surfaced it bites units beside it (40 every 1.6 s) and chews a building beside it (60 every 2 s).
- **Worldbreaker** (Axiarchs): a fault line. Every 3.2 s it slams the ground and a glowing crack races (220 a second, 20 wide) through its target out to 210: everything of the enemy's on the ground along it takes 90, buildings 2.5×; never aircraft. Every 12 s the crack ends in a ridge of floating obsidian slabs 140 long across it, for 5 s: enemy ground units walk around its ends, its own side passes through; it never spans the whole lane, and units standing where it rises are pushed aside.
- **Fix to 2a (v2.83):** nobody steps out of an Artillery shell's marker on its own any more (the AI did in v2.82; armies stopped advancing). Shells still land where aimed, marked, at least 1.6 s later; move units out yourself to dodge. Meteor warnings are still dodged.
Tests: `tests/rework-2b.test.js`; screenshots: `node tests/rework-2b-shots.js` → docs/screenshots/rework2b-*.png.

## Faction rework, batch 3a (v2.84)

The first four divers and melee units of [docs/FACTION_REWORK.md](docs/FACTION_REWORK.md) "Batch 3", behind `REWORK.b3a` (stored per game in `g.rw` like 2a and 2b; on by default; a Test Range row's switch is under Setup → Rework switches, "Batch 3a"; code in src/sim/rework-3a.js, art in src/render/units-rework-3a.js). Each keeps its id and plays through its own per-game definition (`rwD`), so UDEF is untouched.
- **Leaper** (Bloomhost): a parasite. Its leap (140, every 7 s) latches it onto a host: vehicles, walkers and ground units of 4+ supply first, else the heaviest ground unit in reach; never aircraft or buildings. Latched it rides on the host, eats 10 health a second and slows it 30% (up to 3 per host; the slow doesn't add up). It can be shot off (splash at the host hits it too). If the host dies, each Leaper on it hatches 2 temporary Skitterlings (20 s, no supply, no node capture; at most 6 per host).
- **Riftstalker** (Axiarchs): a backline assassin. Its phase-step strike goes for ranged units first and (v2.85) it stays and fights there; a kill chain-blinks it to the next ranged enemy within 130 (up to 2 per strike, no cooldown); its shields breaking while out on a strike, or running out of targets (nothing in reach for 0.5 s), blink it straight back to where it stepped from. The AI strikes with half its shields or more (v2.84: 75%).
- **Parallax** (Axiarchs): a guardian. No leap. When an Axiarch ground ally within 160 drops under 30% health it swaps places with it (the most expensive first; every 10 s; not while itself under 40%; never with an exiled, burrowed or tunnelling unit). Its blows still disrupt.
- **Ravager** (Bloomhost): a devourer. No kill frenzy. Every ground unit that dies leaves remains for 6 s (not Mites or temporary Skitterlings); a Ravager within 60, out of combat or under 60% health, devours them over 1.5 s (no attacks): 30% of its health back and a stack (+10% damage, +10% max health, visibly bigger), up to 3.
With `b3a` off a game plays exactly as v2.83 (the pinned determinism fingerprints match). Tests: `tests/rework-3a.test.js`; screenshots: `node tests/rework-3a-shots.js` → docs/screenshots/rework3a-*.png; self-play: `node audit/sim/selfplay-3a.js [seed]`.

## Faction rework, batch 3b (v2.85)

The last four divers and melee units of [docs/FACTION_REWORK.md](docs/FACTION_REWORK.md) "Batch 3", behind `REWORK.b3b` (stored per game in `g.rw` like the earlier batches; on by default; a Test Range row's switch is under Setup → Rework switches, "Batch 3b"; code in src/sim/rework-3b.js, art in src/render/units-rework-3b.js). Each keeps its id and plays through its own per-game definition (`rwD`), so UDEF is untouched.
- **Dreadthorn** (Bloomhost): a charger. No spine burst. With an enemy ground unit 80 to 200 away it charges in a straight line (140 a second, up to 220): every enemy ground unit it passes takes 50 and is knocked 30 aside. A building, an enemy Worldbreaker ridge, a wall or the field's edge stops it early. Then it is dazed for 2 s (no moves, no attacks; spores and stars circle its head). Ready again 14 s after it starts. It picks the line that tramples the most; the AI's needs 3 supply on the line. Tap an enemy within 200 to aim the next charge at it.
- **Bastion** (Axiarchs): a shield barrier. No shield surge (and no brace). A curved wall of light 70 in front of it, 90 wide across the lane, facing the nearest enemy it sees: enemy shots that would pass through it (bullets, missiles, bolts, beams, building guns) hit the wall instead; lobbed shells, bombs, ground attacks (eruptions, cracks, flame) and melee get past, and its own side's shots pass out. 600 health, back 30 a second after 3 s unhit; broken, it collapses for 8 s and returns at full health. Its pulse (range 100) is unchanged.
- **Shardling** (Axiarchs): a duelist. Cost unchanged. Every 4 s it is ready to parry (a glint on its blade): the next damaging hit, splash too, is negated in full; if the attacker is in melee with it, it strikes back at once for 2× its damage.
- **Bulwark** (Bloomhost): keeps its brace (now its own). On its own creep it regenerates 8 health a second even in combat, and 3× the usual creep regeneration out of combat (its glow sacs pulse).
- **Part 0, the Riftstalker (batch 3a):** see above: it no longer fades back after every strike.
- **AI:** Bastions keep in front of the side's ranged units (which wait behind them on the attack), Bulwarks defending hold on their creep (they chase only what is on it), Dreadthorns charge through groups (never at one small unit); their own scores and persona tastes.
With `b3b` off the four play exactly as v2.84 (the pinned determinism fingerprints match; a seeded skirmish hashes as on v2.84's build). Tests: `tests/rework-3b.test.js`; screenshots: `node tests/rework-3b-shots.js` → docs/screenshots/rework3b-*.png; self-play: `node audit/sim/selfplay-3b.js [seed] [maxT] [predator]`.

## Editing and building (v2.57)

The game ships as one self-contained `index.html`, but you edit it in `src/`:

- `src/head.html`, `src/style.css`, `src/body.html`: the page head, the CSS, and the markup up to `<script>`.
- `src/sim/`: rules, combat and AI (it ends with the `SIM END` marker the tests slice by).
- `src/render/`: drawing (ends with `RENDER END`). `src/ui/`: HUD, input, menus, report, sound, main loop, Codex, tutorial.
- `src/end.html`: the closing tags.

After editing, run `npm run build` (or `node build.js`). It joins the parts in the order listed in `build.js`, byte for byte, with no dependencies and no transforms, and writes `index.html`. Commit `src/` and `index.html` together: GitHub Pages serves `index.html` as it is. `npm run check` (and `npm test`, in `tests/build.test.js`) fails if `index.html` isn't exactly a fresh build of `src/`, or if a file in `src/` isn't listed in `build.js`. To add a file, create it in `src/` and add it to `PARTS` in `build.js` at the right place. Everything shares one global scope, as before, so order matters.

The SIM tests load the SIM section of `index.html` in Node with no dependencies. The browser test needs Playwright (`npm i -D playwright && npx playwright install chromium`) and skips itself if it isn't installed.

Other tools:

- `node tests/balance.js [matches=20] [ai,rush,turtle] [difficulties]`: win counts, timeouts and match lengths for AI vs AI and bot vs AI.
- `node tests/ladder.js [matches=20] [rows]`: the difficulty ladder (AI vs AI for each pair, sides swapped halfway) and every bot against every difficulty.
