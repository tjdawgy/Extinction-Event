# Batch 6f, Part D2: the map fixes

Two changes were tried, each aimed at a cause from D1 (audit/logs-p1/lanes6f-d1/D1.md), each measured with every switch on over
the persona-balanced design (162 Hard vs Hard matches, seeds 600+). One was rejected; one ships. Baseline: Part B
(audit/logs-p1/selfplay6f-b/).

## Change 1 (rejected): the Bloomhost swarm keeps to one lane

Cause 1 (Bloomhost's AI splitting its army on Three-Lane). The Bloomhost AI on lane maps took batch 6e's one-army lane rule.
Bloomhost on Three-Lane 28% → 36%, but it then lost every Three-Lane match to Last Fist (0-15-3) while beating the Axiarchs 13-4;
Last Fist on Three-Lane 69%, the Axiarchs 25% there and 41% overall. Removed (audit/logs-p1/selfplay6f-d2-rejected/REJECTED.md).

## Change 2 (ships, under b6fai): a press attack counts the base's defences (`press6f`, `B6FAI.press` 1)

Cause 2 (Last Fist 0-12 against a Turtle on Classic): the Hard AI's press rule launched all-in and siege pushes on army
strength alone, skipping the engage check and, unlike every other base-attack rule, never counting the base's known defences.
With b6fai on, a press base attack also needs its army at least (the enemy army it knows of + knownDefense: the HQ gun and
known Turrets) × 1. Every AI, every map.

Every faction-map cell is now between 36% and 53%. The effect is not where the change aimed: Last Fist against a Turtle on Classic
went only 0-12 → 2-10 (its first attack is no earlier, 2:24, and it still attacks 18-19 times a match there); Last Fist's
Classic gain came from the other personalities (Boomer 6-6 → 8-3-1). Bloomhost's Three-Lane rise (28 → 42%: 3-6-3 → 5-7 against
the Turtle, 2-9-1 → 3-7-2 against the Economist, 5-7 → 7-5 against the Boomer) has no mechanism in this change beyond press
attacks changing (any AI rule moves every later decision). Read it as one 162-match draw; the 324-match confirmation decides
(audit/logs-p1/selfplay6f-final/).
