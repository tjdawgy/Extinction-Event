Last Fist was at 61% (52-70) after Part B (selfplay6e-b3), above 58%, so Part C ran. Runs: `node audit/sim/early6e.js --out
audit/logs-p1/early6e-c1` on the Part B build (the 162 persona-balanced matches played to 6:00: army value, units by type, income
and nodes at 2:00-5:00, every unit lost); `node audit/sim/early6e-report.js` (report.md) and `node audit/sim/early6e-join.js

games). The first field fight: the first cluster of units lost 250+ from both HQs (a cluster ends when 20 s pass with none lost)
in which 300+ value is lost (smaller ones were scouts).

## The cause

1. **Against the Axiarchs the opening decides**, and it is Last Fist's army size at 4:00, not the first fight (Last Fist loses
   two thirds of those): 1.2× bigger at 4:00 (33 of 54 matches) it wins 25-6; 1.2× smaller, 0-7. The size is the Marines: 13.5
   of them, 39% of the 4:00 army by value, the cheapest volume in the game (45 credits, no energy).
2. **Against Bloomhost it does not**: 6d's reading (the first field fight at 3:00-4:00 and the nodes with it) no longer holds.
   Last Fist wins 15-3 after losing the first fight, 14-3 with fewer nodes at 5:00 and 14-1 with armies within 20% at 4:00. What
   it has over both opponents is volume for the whole match: it spends 11% more a minute on units than Bloomhost and 28% more than
   the Axiarchs (1,389 and 1,401 a minute against 1,246 and 1,098; full matches, selfplay6e-b3), from a little more income (1,983
   and 2,029 a minute against 1,911 and 1,887) and a larger share of it on units (69-70% against 65% and 58%). Its army leads
   Bloomhost's from 5:00 (2,050 / 1,455) and Bloomhost never catches up (2,850 / 1,750 at 9:00, 3,995 / 2,360 at 11:00), though
   Bloomhost trades better per credit (killed value per 100 spent 92 against 82).
3. Node income is not the lever: it decides neither pairing on its own (Bloomhost row above) and is the same rule for every
   faction.

**Lever:** the Marine's price. It is the base of Last Fist's volume (9,624 Marines trained in Part B's run, 433,080 credits; 164
damage and 57 killed value per 100 spent, its best-trading front-line unit) and of its 4:00 army. Prefer the price to its
damage: the cause is how many it fields, not how hard each hits. The Tank, Artillery and Salvage Yard (6d's values) are left alone:
C1 does not point at them.
