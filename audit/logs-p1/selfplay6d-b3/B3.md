## Why the target is not met, and the next lever

Four values were the limit. Last Fist (62%) is 4 points above 58% and the Axiarchs (28%) 14 below 42%. On Classic all three are
within 39-50%; the Axiarchs lose on the lane maps (17% on each), where an army of about 15 units (their median peak) is split across
two or three lanes and the AI's lane play (reinforcing a lane, capture groups, forward pads) was built around larger armies. That is
AI behaviour, which Part B may not change. **Next lever:** the Axiarch AI on lane maps (keep their army on one lane and move it as
one; `aiLanes`/`aiLanes3`), then their energy squeeze (their units are 26% energy by cost against 19% for the other factions, so they
bank credits with idle production: an Axiarch Reactor yield or their units' energy costs).
