## Reading

The fix works through the warp point: lone deaths fell by more than half on both maps and the warp point stopped sitting ahead
of a holding army (on Three-Lane its median moved from the centre node, 1,049 from the HQ, to the base field, 216). The army was
already nearly whole on one lane; it is a little more so on Three-Lane (95%, the second capture group gone). The Axiarchs went
from 17% to 39% and 47% on the lane maps (target 40% on each: Three-Lane met, Two-Lane one point short, inside its interval).
The wins came mostly from Bloomhost (Three-Lane: Bloomhost vs Axiarchs 12-4-2 → 5-10-3), which falls to 35% overall.
