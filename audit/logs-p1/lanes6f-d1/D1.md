## Causes, ranked

1. **Bloomhost's AI splits its swarm on Three-Lane** (its 22-28% there; Last Fist's and the Axiarchs' wins over it). Of every
   faction and map it spreads its army the most (1.38 lanes holding 20%+ of it; the top lane 86%, others 91-95%) and puts the
   most into capture and raid groups (7%, others 1-4%), so it is outnumbered in its main lane in 59% of samples (Last Fist
   24%) and loses 40% of its value outnumbered (Last Fist 23%). Its army falls behind from 8:00 (1,768 to 2,595) though its
   income and nodes keep up (5.1 vs 4.8 nodes at 8:00, 7.3 vs 4.6 at 12:00). The swarm's own mechanic, surge, pays only for a
   packed swarm. An AI fault on one map, not a unit problem: the same units win 67% on Classic.
2. **Last Fist's press attacks into a Turtle on Classic** (its 31% there; Bloomhost's 67%). By opponent personality, Last
   Fist on Classic is 6-6 against a Boomer, 5-7 against an Economist and **0-12 against a Turtle** (6-0 for the Bloomhost
   Turtle, 6-0 for the Axiarch one); every other faction, map and personality cell is between 1-7 and 10-2 except Bloomhost's
   Two-Lane 1-7-4 against the Turtle. Those 12 are half its Classic losses: without them it would be 11-13. The traces show
   one pattern: Last Fist is ahead early on volume (1,945 against 1,024 at 4:10; 3,348 against 855 at 5:10), its Hard AI
   launches all-ins into the Turtle's base again and again (5-6 a match; the first at 4:10), loses 9,500-10,400 by 10:00
   against the Turtle's 6,600-6,700, and the Turtle, which wins the trades at home and out-earns it (Last Fist holds 1.0 nodes
   to 1.6 from 8:00, income 32 against 41), out-grows it and wins at 15:00-17:00. The rule doing it is `press`
   (ATTACK.press): an all-in or siege whenever its army beats the enemy army it knows of by the press edge; it skips the
   engage check and, unlike every other base-attack rule (`thV + hqVal`), never counts the base's known defences (its
   Turrets and HQ gun). On the long lane maps the same pushes meet the Turtle's army in the field instead (Last Fist 6-5-1 and
   5-3-4 against it there). Defending is then Last Fist's largest and worst trade bucket on Classic (0.76: 5,162 lost a
   match against 3,944 killed; 26% of its losses at home, 55% under a minute old) and its army never grows (peak supply 36,
   the lowest of any faction and map).
3. **Supply on the big maps** (a smaller factor): Last Fist's Hard AI sells spare buildings for Habitats and fields 77-100
   supply on Three-Lane (army limit 120), which no other faction does as much (Bloomhost peaks at 53; in a sampled match it
   fields 29 of a 56 cap), so Last Fist's volume edge (6e, C1) grows with the map. Classic's limit (80) is never reached
   (at the cap 13-22% of minutes after 6:00).
